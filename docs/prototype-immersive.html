Adivaram
Act 1 · Thamarassery Churam

Nine bends to the top.

Fourteen kilometres from Adivaram to Lakkidi. The British needed a Paniya guide to find this road. His name was Karinthandan.

Through the arch. You are in Wayanad.

Act 2 · Lakkidi to Kalpetta

Everything is under an hour from here.

The Chain Tree, Vythiri's tree houses, Pookode, then the hub.

Southtea, peaks, falls
Eastcaves, Bathery, forest
Northdam, island, temples
Act 3 · Soochipara Falls

You hear it long before you see it.

Thirteen km from Meppadi, then 1.2 km on foot. Two hundred metres of water in three tiers. Swim when the flow is low.

Act 4 · Kuruva Island

The Kabini splits into a hundred and fifty islands.

Nobody lives on them. The only way in is a bamboo raft, and fewer than five hundred people a day are allowed.

Act 5 · Edakkal Caves

Someone carved these six thousand years ago.

A kilometre of steps, a stone wedged between two stones, and a wall nobody can fully read. Closed Mondays.

Act 7 · The forest road

From here the road belongs to the forest.

Twenty km of protected teak and bamboo. Night traffic banned since 2009. Ninety-three animals died here in the five years before.

An elephant is crossing. Stay in the car.
Act 8 · Muthanga and the border

Kerala ends here.

The road goes on to Bandipur and Mysore, but Wayanad stops at this barrier. Ninety kilometres from Adivaram.
